How it works

Pen position, pressure and buttons come from the tablet's normal pen reports. The coil signals are read from the tablet's memory through the same read-only window the flasher uses to check firmware (feature reports 0xD4 / 0xD1): the frame Wacom's position calculation last received, holding the signal strength of the 10 X and 10 Y coils around the pen. Nothing is written to the tablet.

Coil numbers are converted to tablet coordinates with a fit against the pen reports, refined live (shown under the tablet as counts per coil). Recordings are JSON files with every pen report and coil sample and their times; they can be replayed here at any speed.

Don't use this page and the flasher on the tablet at the same time.
