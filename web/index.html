About & disclaimer

This is an unofficial, community-made tool and firmware for the Wacom Intuos Pro M (PTH-660). It isn't made, approved or supported by Wacom.

We've built in every safeguard we could: new firmware only goes into the slot the tablet isn't running, every write is read back and checked before the tablet switches to it, you can back up both slots first, and Wacom's original firmware is included so you can always go back.

Still, flashing firmware always carries some risk, and custom firmware may affect your warranty. You flash at your own risk: the author can't be held responsible for damaged or bricked tablets, lost data or warranty issues. If that doesn't sit right with you, it's completely fine not to flash; the stock firmware works well.
